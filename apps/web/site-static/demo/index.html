Think out loud. Synapse files it.

Three moments where a thought arrives: on a treadmill, grabbing a coffee, over a burger. Each is said out loud and becomes a real Synapse card (a GitHub issue, a reminder, an email draft). Back at the desk, all three are already written and waiting. The people are illustrations; every Synapse screen and card is the real app, on demo data.
